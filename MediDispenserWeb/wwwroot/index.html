Flow Description

  1. Idle: Device waits. Sends heartbeat every 15 seconds.
  2. Scheduled time arrives (device RTC) or web app sends a manual dispense command.
  3. Pre-check: Pocket must be loaded (IR 2 / loaded-pocket memory). If empty → alert, red LED, stop.
  4. Dispense: Rotate carousel to pocket, open hatch, drop piece, close hatch. Play "time to take your medicine" voice, green LED.
  5. Confirm landing: IR 1 sees the piece. If not → wiggle ±4° and retry (max 3). Still nothing → jam alert.
  6. Wait for patient: Status = waiting.
  7. IR 1 clear (piece removed) → publish taken, play "thank you", back to idle.
  8. Piece put back after being taken → publish returned, play "pill put back".
  9. 5 minutes with no pick-up → publish reminder alert, repeat voice.
  10. 10 minutes (or next scheduled dose arrives first) → publish missed alert, play "dose missed", yellow/red LED.
  11. Back to idle.
